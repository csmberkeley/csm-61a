\question Sally really wants to know what she needs to score to pass her class! Fill out the function \lstinline{score_needed} that takes in $current\_grade$, her current grade, $num\_exams$, the number of exams taken so far (not including her final exam), and $pass\_grade$, the grade needed to pass. Assume that every exam, including the final exam, has the same weight.

def score_needed(current_grade, num_exams, pass_grade):
    '''
    >>> score_needed(89, 4, 90)
    94
    >>> score_needed(65, 2, 70)
    80
    >>> score_needed(77, 10, 78)
    88
    '''
    

\end{lstlisting}
\end{blocksection}

\begin{blocksection}
\begin{solution}[0in]
\begin{lstlisting}
    def score_needed(current_grade, num_exams, pass_grade):
        total_pts = current_grade * num_exams
        total_needed = pass_grade * (num_exams + 1)
        return total_needed - total_pts
\end{lstlisting}
\end{solution}
\end{blocksection}
